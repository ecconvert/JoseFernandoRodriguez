Gallery Style Comparison

Same 65 photos shown in four different styles. Scroll through, click any photo to enlarge, and pick the one that feels right.

Option 1

Scrapbook · Polaroid Scatter

Photos as Polaroid cards on textured paper, slightly rotated, with little tape pieces — a family album feel.

Best for: warmth, personality, storyteller energy  ·  Watch: can feel busy with 65 photos

Option 2

Masonry · Pinterest-Style Grid

A flowing wall where photos keep their natural shapes. Fast to scan, elegant, and lets visitors find what catches their eye.

Best for: elegance, visitor browsing, large galleries  ·  Watch: less emotional than scrapbook

Option 3

Cinematic · Vertical Scroll

One photo at a time, full width, with breathing room between. Slow, deliberate, like turning the pages of a tribute book.

Best for: emotional impact, narrative order  ·  Watch: long page; visitors must scroll a lot

Option 4

Timeline · Grouped by Life Era

Photos organized into chapters of his life. Tells a story chronologically. (Era assignments below are placeholders — you'd sort photos into the right buckets.)

Best for: storytelling, biography  ·  Watch: requires you to sort photos by date
